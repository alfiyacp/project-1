Conclusion¶

The EDA shows that students' social media usage is related to their mental health, study habits, and sleep patterns. Univariate analysis helped understand each variable individually, while bivariate analysis identified relationships between variables. Overall, the insights suggest that maintaining balanced social media usage and healthy daily habits can support better mental well-being among students.
